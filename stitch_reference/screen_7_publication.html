AbstractMethodological Preview
Modern multi-region cloud infrastructures frequently fail deterministically under non-linear cascading partition states, yet standard telemetry frameworks obfuscate true recovery mechanics behind moving percentile medians. In this monograph, we introduce a formal verification framework evaluating state reconstruction across 120 Latin-hypercube trials at 50,000 ingress requests per second. By capturing synchronous eBPF socket traces alongside autonomous control-plane rerouting, the findings substantiate that deterministically orchestrated peer failover achieves an uncompromised availability threshold under severe simulated partition.
Confirmed Empirical Findings (N=120 Trials):
  • 92.4% MTTR Delta: Autonomous deterministic failover restores state in 1.42s vs 18.7s baseline manual orchestration.
  • 99.4% Availability Floor: No catastrophic cascade observed at sustained 50,000 req/s ingress during AZ-1 isolation.
  • Zero Data Loss: Consensus log replication preserved complete sequence continuity (0 uncommitted writes).
